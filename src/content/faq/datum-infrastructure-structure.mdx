---
question: "How is Datum's infrastructure structured?"
order: 7
category: "features"
---

Datum deploys infrastructure in the top internet aggregation points globally. These points of presence (PoPs) are packaged into Regions and Availability Zones (AZs). Each Region represents a specific geographic and network boundary, while each AZ provides independent capacity within that Region.

See [Locations](https://www.datum.net/docs/platform/locations) in the docs for the naming convention, the current list of regions, and how to check what's available to your project.
