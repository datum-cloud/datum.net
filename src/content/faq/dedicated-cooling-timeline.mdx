---
question: 'How does cooling choice affect deployment timeline or location options?'
order: 7
category: 'dedicated-cloud'
---

Air-cooled deployments open up more sites and generally move faster, since most facilities can already support them. Liquid cooling narrows the list to facilities with (or able to add) CDU infrastructure, which can add time if a site needs retrofitting first. There's no universal answer — it comes down to your GPU generation, rack density, and how much timeline flexibility you have, which we work through together during scoping.
