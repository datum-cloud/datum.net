It depends mainly on GPU generation availability, whether you need liquid cooling, and whether an existing facility already fits or needs retrofitting — deploying into ready capacity at a qualified site is far faster than standing up new capacity from scratch. Rather than quote a generic number that won't hold for every configuration, we give you a firm timeline during the scoping call once we know what you're actually building.
