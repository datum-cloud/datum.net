Yes — we're not there to replace a capable in-house team, we're there to add the hardware sourcing, data center relationships, and financing muscle that even a strong infra team usually doesn't have in-house. Plenty of our customers keep full operational control of the cluster and just lean on us for the parts — sourcing, supply chain, site selection — that are hard to build internally.
