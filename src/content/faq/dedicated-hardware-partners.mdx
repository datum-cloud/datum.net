We work across named hardware vendors — Dell, Supermicro, Cisco — plus a network of data center operators and network providers, rather than owning the stack ourselves. That independence means the cluster gets designed around your requirements instead of whatever happens to be sitting in a single vendor's inventory, and you get one point of contact instead of five separate vendor relationships to manage.
