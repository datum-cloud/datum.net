---
question: 'Why liquid cooling, and what does that mean for site selection?'
order: 4
category: 'dedicated-cloud'
---

Current-generation racks like the NVL72 run at power densities air cooling simply can't dissipate efficiently, so liquid cooling — direct-to-chip or rear-door heat exchange — becomes a requirement rather than an option. That narrows the field: not every facility has the CDU plumbing and power infrastructure to support it, so site selection becomes as much about a data center's cooling readiness as its geography. We factor that into sourcing from day one so you're not stuck discovering it after a lease is signed.
