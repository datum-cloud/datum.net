question: 'Can I choose where my cluster is physically located?'
order: 8
category: 'dedicated-cloud'
---

Yes. Tell us the region, jurisdiction, latency, or power requirements that matter for your workload, and we source data center capacity from our partner network to fit — rather than placing you wherever we happen to have inventory. Cooling requirements factor in too: your GPU generation and cooling choice may narrow the realistic options, and we'll walk through that trade-off with you directly.
