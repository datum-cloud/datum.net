---
question: 'How is a single NVL72 rack put together?'
order: 3
category: 'dedicated-cloud'
---

An NVL72 rack pairs 72 Blackwell-generation GPUs with Grace CPUs in a single rack-scale system, connected by NVLink and NVSwitch so all 72 GPUs behave like one large accelerator rather than 72 separate ones. That density draws far more power per rack than air cooling can dissipate, which is why NVL72 deployments are liquid-cooled by default. We handle the rack design, hardware sourcing, and cooling integration as one package rather than leaving you to reconcile compute, networking, and thermal requirements on your own.
