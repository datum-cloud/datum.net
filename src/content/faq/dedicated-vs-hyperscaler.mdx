---
question: 'How is this different from renting GPU capacity from a hyperscaler?'
order: 11
category: 'dedicated-cloud'
---

A hyperscaler rents you shared, multi-tenant capacity out of a fixed catalog of instance types and regions — you take what's available. A Datum cluster is built and dedicated to you: GPU generation, networking, cooling, storage, and location are all chosen to fit your workload rather than fit a shared inventory, and our team is involved in the design from day one rather than handing you a self-serve console.
