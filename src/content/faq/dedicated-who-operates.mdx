---
question: "Who operates the cluster once it's live — Datum, or my own team?"
order: 12
category: 'dedicated-cloud'
---

Whichever you prefer. By default we handle day-to-day operations — monitoring, incident response, lifecycle management — so you don't need to build out an ops team just to keep the cluster running. If your team wants to operate it directly instead, the platform layer (Kubernetes, Slurm, observability, and the rest) is there for you to run yourselves, and plenty of teams land somewhere in between.
