---
question: "Why is Datum currently free?"
order: 2
category: "features"
---

We're in public beta, and we want as many builders as possible testing and shaping the platform without billing getting in the way. Every feature is free to use right now — no feature gates, no seat limits. When we do introduce billing, we'll give plenty of advance notice and a grace period to review rates before anything changes — you won't get a surprise bill.
