## Why is pricing so complicated?

Here's how we make it simple: no feature gates, no seat limits, no hidden fees.

Every rate we charge is published on this page, per unit, across Deliver, Build, Connect, and Data
transfer. Ingress to our network is always free, egress starts free up to 200 GB a month, and
billing is per-second — you pay only while something is running.

Datum is free during public beta. When that ends we will give you advance notice and a grace
period to review the rates before any billing begins.
